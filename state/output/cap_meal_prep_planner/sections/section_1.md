*The quantities below are an illustrative example for four people: seven dinners, four packed lunches, and two make-ahead breakfasts. They are not stored household settings; adjust quantities to your recipes, serving count, pantry, and preferences.*

### Grocery-List Workflow

1. Check the pantry, refrigerator, and freezer before shopping.
2. Compare recipes and combine duplicate ingredients.
3. Move food nearing expiration to a “Use First” area.
4. Delete items already stocked and add household or dietary needs.
5. Shop in order: pantry, bakery, frozen, produce, meat, then dairy.
