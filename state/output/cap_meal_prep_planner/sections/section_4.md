## Weekly Schedule

The example below shows a practical week for a busy household of four. Adjust portions and times as needed while keeping prep, leftovers, and freezer use organized.

### Weekly Meal and Prep Schedule

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Daily Prep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Overnight oats with berries and nuts | Turkey-hummus wraps with carrots | Sheet-pan chicken, broccoli, and sweet potatoes | Cook grains, roast vegetables, shred chicken, and portion snacks. |
| Tuesday | Vegetable egg muffins | Chicken quinoa bowls with cucumber and tomato | Lentil pasta salad with greens | Cook lentil pasta, wash produce, and pack two lunches. |
| Wednesday | Greek yogurt with granola and fruit | Lentil pasta salad leftovers | Black bean tacos with corn and peppers | Make taco filling, cook rice, prepare toppings, and combine taco leftovers. |
| Thursday | Oatmeal with banana and peanut butter | Black bean taco bowls | Teriyaki salmon with rice and broccoli | Cook salmon and vegetables, make rice, and portion leftovers. |
| Friday | Bagels with cream cheese and fruit | Salmon rice bowls | Vegetable pizza with side salad | Prepare pizza toppings and dressing; wash and store remaining salad ingredients. |
| Saturday | Spinach-feta pancakes with berries | Vegetable pizza leftovers | Build-your-own taco bar | Make pancake batter, check refrigerator inventory, and move suitable freezer items forward. |
| Sunday | Vegetable frittata with toast | Taco bar leftovers | Vegetable soup with grilled sandwiches | Use remaining vegetables, review supplies, identify expiring foods, and plan next week’s meals. |

### Daily Closing Checklist

- [x] Refrigerate perishables within two hours.
- [x] Date and label cooked food and leftovers.
- [x] Pack lunches in insulated containers with an ice pack.
- [x] Keep raw proteins sealed and separate from ready-to-eat foods.
- [x] Check the refrigerator before preparing the next day’s meals.
- [x] Freeze remaining cooked portions that will not be eaten within three to four days.
- [x] Clean food-prep surfaces and wash reusable containers.

**Conclusion:** This schedule provides seven complete days of varied meals while using batch cooking, planned leftovers, and short daily prep sessions to keep the week manageable for a busy household.
