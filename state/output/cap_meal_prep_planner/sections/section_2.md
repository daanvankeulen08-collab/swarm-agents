## Prep Checklist

Use this checklist to plan meals, shop efficiently, complete one focused prep session, and store leftovers safely. The example serves a household of four; adjust quantities to match your family size and schedule.

### 1. Weekly Planning Snapshot

| Planning Item | Example Decision |
|---|---|
| People feeding | 4 |
| Prep day | Saturday, 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. |
| Refrigerator space | Clear two shelves and one drawer |
| Freezer space | Confirm containers fit before shopping |
| Main meals planned | 7 dinners plus 5 packed lunches |
| Budget target | $125 |
| Dietary needs | Nut-free meals |
| Flexible meal | Friday salmon may be swapped |

### 2. Example Weekly Menu

| Day | Main Meal | Batch-Prep Action | Portions | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Monday | Mediterranean chicken bowls | Cook chicken, quinoa, cucumber, and dressing | 4 | Planned |
| Tuesday | Leftover chicken bowls | Pack refrigerated ingredients separately | 4 | Planned |
| Wednesday | Chicken taco jars | Divide seasoned chicken, beans, corn, and slaw | 4 | Planned |
| Thursday | Leftover taco jars | Refrigerate jars with dressing packed separately | 4 | Planned |
| Friday | Baked salmon and roasted vegetables | Roast salmon; prepare vegetables earlier in the week | 4 | Planned |
| Saturday | Vegetable pasta with marinara | Cook pasta and sauce; pack grated cheese separately | 4 | Planned |
| Sunday | Roast chicken and mixed vegetables | Roast chicken and vegetables; save leftovers for Monday’s lunch | 4 | Planned |

### 4. Prep-Day Checklist

| Time | Task | Completion Standard | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before starting | Wash hands and clear workspace | Counters sanitized; cutting boards ready | ☐ |
| Before starting | Preheat oven and set timers | Oven at 425°F; timers visible | ☐ |
| First task | Wash and chop vegetables | Uniform pieces stored separately by recipe | ☐ |
| First task | Cook grains and pasta | Properly cooked, chilled, and portioned | ☐ |
| Main task | Season and roast proteins | Chicken reaches 165°F internally | ☐ |
| Main task | Cook beans and finalize sauces | Ingredients labeled with dates | ☐ |
| Final task | Assemble meal-prep containers | Complete portions packed without overfilling | ☐ |
| Final task | Refrigerate and freeze promptly | Cold food placed at or below 40°F | ☐ |
| Final task | Clean and reset | Surfaces cleaned; perishables put away | ☐ |

### 5. Storage and Safety Check

| Check | Standard | Check |
|---|---|:---:|
| Refrigeration | Refrigerate perishables within two hours | ☐ |
| Cooling | Spread large portions shallowly before refrigerating | ☐ |
| Separation | Keep raw meat sealed and away from produce | ☐ |
| Labeling | Add meal name and prep or use-by date | ☐ |
| Freezing | Freeze meals intended for later weeks at 0°F | ☐ |
| Reheating | Heat leftovers to 165°F before serving | ☐ |

**Conclusion:** A complete menu, grouped shopping list, timed prep schedule, and storage check turn meal planning into a manageable routine. Completing these steps on the designated prep day makes weekday meals and packed lunches faster, calmer, and easier to enjoy.
