## Certification Deadlines

Use this section to track professional certifications, required trainings, screening documents, and hospital/clinic privileges. Renewal dates and rules vary by credentialing body and facility; verify them directly with the issuer and each client. All dates below are illustrative examples, not actual deadlines.

## Weekly Certification Check

- [ ] Monday: Check certification portals for exam results, policy notices, or continuing-education requirements.
- [ ] Tuesday: Review background check, drug screening, and TB screening status.
- [ ] Wednesday: Log new continuing-education courses, certificates, and credits.
- [ ] Thursday: Confirm that each hospital or clinic has current orientation and access documents.
- [ ] Friday: Review deadlines falling within the next 30, 60, and 90 days.
- [ ] Add every completed course or renewed credential to the master tracker.
- [ ] Upload the completion certificate or confirmation receipt to the appropriate folder.
- [ ] Notify the facility credentialing contact before a credential expires.

## Monthly and Quarterly Review

- [ ] Verify whether any certification has changed its renewal cycle.
- [ ] Compare personal credentials with the strictest requirement among current clients.
- [ ] Update expiration dates after receiving a renewed certificate.
- [ ] Reconcile recorded continuing-education credits with the issuer’s account.
- [ ] Check facility-specific requirements for each active assignment.
- [ ] Set reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 14 days before expiration.
- [ ] Escalate missing credentials before the final submission deadline.

## Status Key

- **Complete:** Requirement finished and documentation saved.
- **In progress:** Started, but documentation or approval is pending.
- **Not started:** No action has begun.
- **Expiring soon:** Deadline is within 30 days.
- **Action required:** The issuer or facility reported missing, expired, or noncompliant documentation.

## Conclusion

A weekly review keeps freelance medical interpreters eligible for every assignment. Maintaining one master deadline list, verifying facility-specific requirements early, and storing proof immediately reduces the risk of lapsed credentials and interrupted hospital or clinic work.
