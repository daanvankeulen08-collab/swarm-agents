### Assignment Readiness Checklist

**Before accepting or changing an assignment**

- [ ] Confirm date, start/end time, language, specialty, setting, and interpretation modality.
- [ ] Verify reporting location, arrival procedure, parking, security requirements, and cancellation deadline.
- [ ] Confirm coordinator name and after-hours escalation contact.
- [ ] Check contract, payment rate, approved expenses, invoicing method, and timesheet deadline.
- [ ] Verify required credentials, orientation, background check, liability coverage, and site access.
- [ ] Review travel time and identify a backup route.
- [ ] Prepare neutral professional clothing, charger, water, identification, and required equipment.
- [ ] Store documents securely; use approved passwords and multifactor authentication.
- [ ] Reconfirm the schedule the evening before and upon arrival.
- [ ] Decline or clarify assignments that create unsafe travel, unclear conditions, or credential concerns.

**After every shift**

- [ ] Record hours, mileage, expenses, and schedule changes.
- [ ] Notify the coordinator promptly about delays, cancellations, access problems, or safety concerns.
- [ ] Submit timesheets and invoices by the agreed deadline.
- [ ] Follow up on missing documentation, payment, or replacement badges.
- [ ] Document any privacy or safety incident according to agency policy, without including patient details.
