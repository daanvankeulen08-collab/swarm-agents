Client Database Checklists ### New Client Onboarding - [ ] Assign a unique client ID. - [ ] Record organization, department, address, and service type. - [ ] Verify scheduling and billing contacts. - [ ] Record communication preferences. - [ ] Document language, credential, equipment, dress-code, and access requirements. - [ ] Check contract, purchase-order, invoicing, and payment terms. - [ ] Schedule credential submission and acknowledgment. - [ ] Confirm the first assignment and reporting contact. - [ ] Review confidentiality and data-handling rules. - [ ] Set a follow-up date for verification. ### Weekly Maintenance - [ ] Confirm every upcoming assignment. - [ ] Record cancellations, replacements, time changes, and new contacts. - [ ] Review pending follow-ups and overdue confirmations. - [ ] Check credential and contract expiration dates. - [ ] Reconcile hours with timesheets. - [ ] Verify invoices and payment status. - [ ] Update inactive or duplicate records. - [ ] Confirm that no patient information is stored. ### Client Offboarding - [ ] Confirm final assignment completion.
