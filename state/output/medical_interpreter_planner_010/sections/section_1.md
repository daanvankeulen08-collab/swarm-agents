### Daily Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Review the schedule, assignment reference, and confirmation details.
- [ ] Confirm modality, language, specialty, expected volume, and contact protocol.
- [ ] Check badge, identification, credentials, and parking instructions.
- [ ] Pack charged laptop, power supply, headset, phone, and backup headset.
- [ ] Download required records or terminology lists through approved systems.
- [ ] Verify confidentiality, security, and recording policies.
- [ ] Test virtual platform, camera, microphone, internet connection, and telephone backup.
- [ ] Review maps, traffic, parking, and the required arrival buffer.
- [ ] Notify the coordinator immediately of delays, cancellations, or equipment issues.
- [ ] Record actual start/end times and complete the required encounter documentation.
- [ ] Remove patient information from working notes according to security policy.
- [ ] Submit billing documentation and save confirmation.

### End-of-Week Checklist

- [ ] Reconcile each assignment’s scheduled, actual, and billable hours.
- [ ] Follow up on pending confirmations and missing encounter numbers.
- [ ] Submit billing packets by contract deadlines.
- [ ] Back up records to an approved, encrypted location.
- [ ] File mileage and travel expenses where permitted.
- [ ] Note recurring facilities, parking delays, and scheduling conflicts.
- [ ] Request next week’s availability before the required deadline.
- [ ] Identify backup coverage for high-risk or tightly buffered assignments.
- [ ] Mark unavailable periods clearly on the shared calendar.

**Conclusion:** The Weekly Appointment Grid provides one coordinated view of clinical assignments, travel, preparation, compliance, and administrative follow-up. Used with the detailed assignment sheet and checklists, it helps freelance medical interpreters reduce missed details, protect realistic transition time, and close each work week accurately.
