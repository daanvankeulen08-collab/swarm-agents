### Assignment and evidence checklist

Complete this checklist for **every active assignment** during weekly planning:

- [ ] Record the client, facility, department, assignment start/end dates, work location/state, scheduler, and facility credentialing contact.
- [ ] Locate the governing contract, facility credentialing policy, applicable law/regulation, and credential-issuer requirements.
- [ ] Copy the **exact requirement** and identify its source, section, revision date, and applicability.
- [ ] Confirm the requirement with the relevant official regulator, facility, or issuer; save the URL/document or written response.
- [ ] Record the credential holder, issue date, expiration date, renewal window, and renewal trigger.
- [ ] Log the verification date, verifier, source/contact, and evidence location.
- [ ] Mark a requirement “not applicable” only with a documented reason and evidence; remove it from the active view while retaining the audit record.
- [ ] Assign one named operational owner, current status, specific next action, and internal due date.
- [ ] Review newly assigned facilities, approaching deadlines, rejected evidence, credential changes, and overdue actions every week.
