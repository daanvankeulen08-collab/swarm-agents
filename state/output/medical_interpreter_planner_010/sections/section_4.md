### Invoice Readiness Checklist

Before sending an invoice:

- [ ] Client name, billing address, and accounts-payable contact are correct.
- [ ] Contract, rate, minimum billing unit, and shift premium are verified.
- [ ] Service date, hours, assignment reference, and delivery method are documented.
- [ ] Required purchase order or cost-center information is attached.
- [ ] Mileage, parking, travel time, and other expenses are contractually allowable.
- [ ] Receipts are saved for every reimbursable expense.
- [ ] Hourly fees, expenses, taxes or adjustments, and total are recalculated.
- [ ] Payment terms and due date match the contract.
- [ ] Invoice number is unique and sequential.
- [ ] Invoice contains no patient name, date of birth, diagnosis, or other unnecessary PHI.
- [ ] A clean PDF is sent through the approved secure method.
- [ ] Copy, delivery confirmation, and follow-up date are saved.

### Payment Follow-Up Schedule

- **Before sending:** Verify supporting records and client requirements.
- **Three business days after sending:** Confirm the invoice was received and routed correctly.
- **Three business days before the due date:** Send a courteous payment reminder if no advance notice is provided.
- **One business day after the due date:** Contact accounts payable and request a payment date.
- **Three to five business days after the due date:** Escalate to the client supervisor or billing manager.
- **After 30 days overdue:** Review the contract, document the balance, and escalate formally; do not suspend services without checking contractual terms.
- **When payment arrives:** Record the date, method, check or transaction number, and remaining balance.

**Conclusion:** A complete register separates submitted, pending, ready-to-send, and overdue invoices while preserving a clear next action for every item. Weekly reconciliation, documented follow-ups, and privacy-safe invoices provide a reliable process for getting freelance medical interpreting payments paid on time.
