## Client Database

The Client Database is the operational directory for hospitals, clinics, sites, contacts, assignments, schedules, and onboarding requirements. Store only the information needed to schedule and invoice work. Do not enter patient names, medical-record numbers, diagnoses, or other protected health information in client notes. The example rows below are fictional and should be replaced with verified records. **Data model:** One client can have multiple sites, contacts, and assignments. Each assignment links to required credentials and a weekly time entry. Use a unique ID for every client, contact, assignment, and document. ### 1.
