## Meal-Prep Checklist

### Sunday: Main Prep Session
- ☐ Cook a batch of quinoa or brown rice.
- ☐ Bake sweet potatoes and a tray of vegetables.
- ☐ Cook and portion the chicken breast.
- ☐ Make turkey chili and freeze extra portions.
- ☐ Prepare four breakfast overnight-oat jars.
- ☐ Wash produce and store it in ready-to-use containers.

### Wednesday: Midweek Reset
- ☐ Refrigerate Monday’s salmon and quinoa bowl for Tuesday.
- ☐ Prepare Wednesday’s tofu stir-fry ingredients.
- ☐ Set Thursday’s taco toppings.
- ☐ Check pantry supplies and replenish snacks.
