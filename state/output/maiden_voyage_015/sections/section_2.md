## Prep Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare a healthy weekly meal plan for a busy household. Nutrition figures below are rounded estimates based on typical ingredients; verify package labels and adjust for allergies or dietary needs.

## 1. Planning Setup

| Checklist Item | Realistic Example | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Planning date | Sunday, 7:00 p.m. | Schedule preparation before the workweek |
| People fed | 4 household members | Confirm portions and preferences |
| Meals required | 4 lunches and 5 dinners | Select recipes that overlap |
| Dietary needs | No peanuts; one vegetarian dinner | Add substitutions and label meals |
| Preferred leftovers | 2 lunches for Thursday | Confirm refrigerator and freezer space |
| Nutrition priority | Protein at main meals | Target approximately 30–40 g per serving |
| Prep time available | 2 hours | Sequence chopping, cooking, and packing |

## 2. Weekly Prep Timeline

| Time | Task | Checklist |
|---|---|---|
| Before shopping | Check inventory | ☐ Inventory refrigerator, freezer, pantry, spices, and storage containers |
| Before shopping | Review the meal plan | ☐ Confirm servings, dietary restrictions, and ingredient overlaps |
| Shopping | Buy ingredients | ☐ Compare package nutrition labels and prices |
| Prep day: first 20 min | Prepare workspace | ☐ Wash hands; sanitize counters; set up cutting boards and containers |
| Prep day: next 40 min | Cook grains and proteins | ☐ Start rice or quinoa; cook proteins safely |
| Prep day: next 30 min | Prepare vegetables | ☐ Chop, roast, steam, or blanch vegetables |
| Prep day: final 20 min | Assemble and store | ☐ Cool food promptly, divide portions, label, and refrigerate or freeze |
| Evening before | Final check | ☐ Confirm every planned meal has a protein, vegetable, and balanced carbohydrate |

| Recipe | Batch Yield | Key Ingredients | Estimated Nutrition per Serving |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Sheet-pan chicken, brown rice, and vegetables | 6 servings | 2 lb chicken breast, 4 cups cooked brown rice, 8 cups mixed vegetables, 2 tbsp olive oil | 455 kcal; 43 g protein; 42 g carbohydrates; 13 g fat; 8 g fiber |
| Turkey and black-bean chili | 8 servings | 2 lb lean ground turkey, 2 cans black beans, 1 can diced tomatoes, vegetables, chili spices | 390 kcal; 36 g protein; 45 g carbohydrates; 9 g fat; 14 g fiber |
| Mediterranean quinoa bowls | 8 servings | 2 cups dry quinoa, 1 can chickpeas, cucumber, peppers, spinach, feta-style cheese | 420 kcal; 17 g protein; 58 g carbohydrates; 12 g fat; 11 g fiber |
| Overnight yogurt and fruit jars | 4 servings | Plain Greek yogurt, oats, berries, chia seeds | 310 kcal; 22 g protein; 42 g carbohydrates; 6 g fat; 8 g fiber |

| Category | Realistic Example Quantity | Checklist |
|---|---:|---|
| Proteins | 2 lb chicken; 2 lb lean turkey | ☐ Compare unit price and lean-protein labels |
| Whole grains | 2 cups dry quinoa or brown rice | ☐ Check grains and tortillas |
| Vegetables | 12 cups mixed vegetables; salad greens | ☐ Choose seasonal, colorful produce |
| Fruit | Berries, apples, oranges | ☐ Buy enough for snacks and breakfast jars |
| Canned goods | Beans, chickpeas, tomatoes | ☐ Select lower-sodium options |
| Dairy or alternatives | Greek yogurt; milk or fortified alternative | ☐ Check protein and added-sugar content |
| Flavorings | Olive oil, herbs, spices, lemon | ☐ Confirm all needed ingredients are available |
| Storage | Eight 2–3 cup containers; four jars | ☐ Count containers before preparation begins |

## 5. Storage and Safety Checklist

- ☐ Refrigerate perishables promptly; keep the refrigerator at **40°F/4°C or below**.
- ☐ Cool cooked food before loosely closing containers.
- ☐ Label each container with the **meal name and preparation date**.
- ☐ Refrigerate meals for the first **3–4 days** of the week.
- ☐ Freeze later portions at **0°F/-18°C**.
- ☐ Thaw frozen food safely in the refrigerator, cold water, or microwave—not on the counter.
- ☐ Reheat meals to **165°F/74°C** before eating.
- ☐ Keep raw proteins separate from vegetables and ready-to-eat foods.

## Conclusion

A successful prep session depends on a verified meal plan, overlapping ingredients, realistic batch sizes, and dated storage. Completing these checklists creates a varied, nutrition-aware week while reducing last-minute decisions and food waste.
