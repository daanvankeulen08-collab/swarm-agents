### Assignment Readiness Checklist

**Before accepting or publishing the schedule**

- [ ] Assignment dates, hours, service, and location match the written invitation.
- [ ] Language, specialty, and interpreter mode requirements are confirmed.
- [ ] Credentialing requirements and expiration dates have been checked.
- [ ] Facility contact and after-hours contact information are recorded in a secure calendar.
- [ ] Travel, parking, badge, portal, and equipment arrangements are confirmed.
- [ ] Overlapping assignments and required rest periods have been checked.
- [ ] On-call windows include a clear acceptance deadline.

**Before each assignment**

- [ ] Review the reporting address, entrance, and check-in time.
- [ ] Confirm the assigned contact or covering coordinator.
- [ ] Test the phone, headset, camera, internet connection, and VPN when applicable.
- [ ] Carry the required photo ID, credentials, and access devices.
- [ ] Review the facility’s confidentiality, safety, and incident-reporting procedures.
- [ ] Arrive early enough to complete security and system checks.
