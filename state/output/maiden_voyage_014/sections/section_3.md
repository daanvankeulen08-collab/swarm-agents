### Abbreviation decoder

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Commonly confused with / confirmation required | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| OD | Right eye | “Overdose” is also used; confirm ocular context and patient perspective | X |
| OS | Left eye | Confirm the complete term; never interpret as “other side” | X |
| OU | Both eyes | Confirm that the body site is the eyes | X |
| qOD | Every other day in some notation | “qd” may mean once daily; verify facility convention | X/M |
| STAT | Immediately | Not necessarily equivalent to routine “urgent”; confirm timing/workflow | X |
| mcg | Microgram | “mg” means milligram; repeat both number and unit | X/M |

**Safety note:** Never decode an unfamiliar, truncated, or context-dependent abbreviation. “Eye l” is incomplete, not valid terminology: obtain the written term, confirm laterality from the patient’s perspective, and escalate uncertainty. Read back medication names, routes, doses, times, procedure sites, and laboratory units before interpreting.

### Laboratory terminology

| Test/result | Interpretation | Unit and reference-interval check | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBC | Complete blood count | Verify specimen, method, units, and site report flags | L |
| WBC: 11.0 × 10⁹/L | White blood cell count: eleven point zero times ten to the ninth per liter | Read the exact unit; do not classify until the site interval/flag is checked | L |
| Creatinine | Laboratory measurement used in kidney assessment | Confirm specimen and mg/dL or µmol/L; use only that site’s interval | L |
| Troponin I | Assay-specific laboratory marker | Confirm assay, unit, collection time, cutoff, and report interval | L |

Reference intervals vary by laboratory, method, population, and reporting policy; never import an interval from another assignment or institution.

### Assignment-readiness checklist

- Expand every abbreviation; stop if unfamiliar or ambiguous.
- Confirm patient-perspective laterality and exact anatomy.
- Capture medication name, route, frequency, dose, and concentration.
- Capture procedure site, laterality, approach, specimen, and anesthesia details.
- Capture laboratory analyte, specimen, unit, reference interval, and flag.
- Record source edition/link, verification date, site-approved term, and verifier/status.
