## Certification Deadlines

Use this section to prevent lapsed credentials, delayed renewals, and ineligible assignments. Certification and credentialing rules vary by jurisdiction, issuing organization, hospital, clinic, payer, and assignment type; confirm every requirement in writing. All dates and organizations below are illustrative examples.

### 2. Renewal Project Plan

| Time Remaining | Required Action | Evidence Stored | Completion Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90 days before expiration | Verify exact renewal requirements and processing time | Issuer notice, facility checklist, or written confirmation | Requirements documented |
| 60 days before expiration | Gather identity information, transcripts, continuing education, and payment | Completed application documents | All materials reviewed |
| 45 days before expiration | Submit renewal or schedule required course/testing | Submission receipt or course confirmation | Submission number recorded |
| 30 days before expiration | Follow up and correct deficiencies | Issuer response and correction receipt | No unresolved issues |
| 14 days before expiration | Escalate to credentialing contact if approval is pending | Email thread and escalation date | Facility confirms temporary status in writing, if available |
| 7 days before expiration | Block future assignments requiring the credential if unresolved | Updated credentialing dashboard | Credential marked current or requirement removed |
| After expiration | Archive expired credential and replacement proof | Final certificate or approval letter | Tracker updated with new expiration date |

### 4. Final Submission Checklist

- [ ] Exact credential name, number, issuer, and expiration date recorded  
- [ ] Renewal window and external deadline confirmed directly  
- [ ] Internal deadline entered at least 60 days before expiration  
- [ ] Continuing-education or transcript requirements documented  
- [ ] Identity, payment, and application materials saved securely  
- [ ] Submission receipt stored in the credential file  
- [ ] 30-, 14-, and 7-day reminders scheduled  
- [ ] Facility-specific requirements checked for every assignment  
- [ ] Replacement certificate or approval letter uploaded  
- [ ] Expired credentials archived without deleting history  
- [ ] No assignment scheduled when a required credential is expired or unverified  

**Conclusion:** A current master tracker, documented facility requirements, and advance renewal reminders allow freelance medical interpreters to protect assignment eligibility and avoid preventable certification gaps.
