## Invoice Tracking

Use this section to verify hours, issue invoices, monitor payment deadlines, reconcile deposits, and prevent missing or duplicate charges. Keep patient names, medical record numbers, and other protected health information off invoices; use assignment or work-order references instead.

**Status key:** Draft = not issued; Sent = delivered but not scheduled; Scheduled = payment date confirmed; Overdue = unpaid after the due date; Paid = payment received, deposited, matched, and cleared.

### 4. Invoice Tracking Checklist

#### Capture and Validation
- [ ] Confirm assignment, department, work-order number, service date, hours, rate, and approved minimums or travel expenses.
- [ ] Compare invoiced hours with the signed timesheet or client-approved record.
- [ ] Check contract-specific billing rates, billing-cycle requirements, and remittance instructions.
- [ ] Confirm invoice descriptions contain no patient identifiers or unnecessary clinical details.

#### Invoice Preparation
- [ ] Use a unique, sequential invoice number.
- [ ] Verify service dates, quantities, rates, subtotal, payment terms, and calculated due date.
- [ ] Attach authorized timesheets or work orders using an approved secure method.
- [ ] Proofread the final invoice and save an identical copy.

#### Submission and Follow-Up
- [ ] Send through the required portal or email and save delivery confirmation.
- [ ] Record the sent date, recipient, portal confirmation, or email reference.
- [ ] Calendar reminders seven days before due date, on the due date, and at 7 and 21 days overdue.
- [ ] Document every follow-up date, contact person, promised payment date, and reference number.
- [ ] Investigate short payments, duplicate payments, incorrect invoice references, and disputed charges promptly.

#### Reconciliation and Weekly Close
- [ ] Match each deposit or payment to the correct invoice.
- [ ] Record payment date, method, reference number, and clearing status.
- [ ] Mark an invoice paid only after the funds have cleared and the amount matches.
- [ ] Move unresolved invoices, disputes, and missing documentation into next week’s action list.

**Conclusion:** This invoice-tracking system provides complete visibility from assignment completion through payment reconciliation, helping prioritize overdue balances, prevent missed revenue, and maintain accurate financial records across every hospital or clinic assignment.
