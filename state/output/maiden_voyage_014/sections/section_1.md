### Per-Assignment Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Confirmation number, facility address, department, contact, and reporting instructions received.
- [ ] Assignment language, modality, expected patient count, and special terminology confirmed.
- [ ] Patient charts, schedules, referral letters, and relevant forms reviewed securely.
- [ ] Professional glossary, abbreviations, and required medical terminology prepared.
- [ ] Badge, parking, entrance, security, and check-in requirements verified.
- [ ] Travel time includes a buffer for traffic, parking, elevators, and badging.
- [ ] Remote technology, audio, camera, lighting, headphones, and backup connection tested.
- [ ] Confidentiality screen, notifications, and surroundings arranged for remote work.
- [ ] Interpreter contact and escalation procedure available.
- [ ] On-call hours and response expectations clearly defined.

### End-of-Day Closeout Checklist

- [ ] Actual start, finish, break, and travel times recorded.
- [ ] Overtime, on-call time, mileage, parking, and other approved expenses documented.
- [ ] Facility confirmation or attendance evidence saved securely.
- [ ] Invoicing platform updated and submission deadline checked.
- [ ] Follow-up requests, chart questions, and delayed reports sent.
- [ ] Temporary notes downloaded securely and paper materials removed from the facility.
- [ ] Next day’s assignments, preparation needs, and travel buffer reviewed.
