### 4. Daily Assignment Checklist

**Before the assignment**
- [ ] Confirm the assignment ID, schedule, reporting location, and primary contact.
- [ ] Review supervisor instructions and expected interpreter role.
- [ ] Prepare relevant terminology, abbreviations, and proper names.
- [ ] Verify identification, badge, system access, and equipment.
- [ ] Confirm confidentiality, safety, and emergency procedures.

**During the assignment**
- [ ] Arrive early enough to complete sign-in and setup.
- [ ] Clarify unfamiliar, opaque, context-dependent, or polysemous terms.
- [ ] Remain within the professional interpreter role and assignment scope.
- [ ] Communicate schedule, workflow, and technical problems promptly.
- [ ] Record only the minimum operational information needed for follow-up.
- [ ] Never place protected health information in this planner.

**After the assignment**
- [ ] Confirm that all requested services were completed.
- [ ] Return badges, equipment, keys, or secure documents.
- [ ] Document unresolved issues, responsible contacts, and deadlines.
- [ ] Remove or securely destroy temporary sensitive material.
- [ ] Submit required attendance or time confirmation.
- [ ] Carry unfinished actions into the handoff table.
