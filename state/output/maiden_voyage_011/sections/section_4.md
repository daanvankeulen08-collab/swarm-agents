## Invoice Tracking

Use one register row per invoice and review it weekly. Record billing details against the correct hospital, clinic, or assignment without including patient names, diagnoses, medical-record numbers, or other protected health information (PHI). All examples below are illustrative; contract and purchase-order terms take precedence.

### Invoice Checklist

**Before issuing**

- [ ] Confirm client legal name, billing address, contact, and payment method.
- [ ] Match service dates, hours, assignment, and purchase order to source records.
- [ ] Verify contractual rate, approved overtime, reimbursable expenses, and total.
- [ ] Confirm invoice number, issue date, payment terms, due date, and remittance instructions.
- [ ] Remove all PHI and patient-identifying details.
- [ ] Send through an approved secure method and save delivery confirmation.

**After issuing and weekly review**

- [ ] Record invoice in the register on the submission date.
- [ ] Reconcile payments against bank or client-portal records.
- [ ] Set reminders seven, three, and one day before the due date.
- [ ] Document every client call, dispute, promise to pay, and follow-up date.
- [ ] Apply late fees or collection terms only when permitted by the contract.
- [ ] Mark paid invoices closed only after funds are confirmed.
- [ ] Store invoices and supporting records securely, with access controls, backups, and contractually required retention.

**Conclusion:** The register provides complete visibility into issued, overdue, disputed, partially paid, and draft invoices while supporting consistent follow-up, accurate cash-flow expectations, and secure medical-interpreter billing practices.
