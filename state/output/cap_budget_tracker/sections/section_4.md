## Tax Checklist

Use this checklist to organize estimated payments, deductible records, and annual filing tasks for a freelancer with irregular income. The examples below are illustrative; tax rules, deadlines, and expense eligibility vary by jurisdiction.

### 1. Tax Profile Setup

| Status | Task | Record or Evidence | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Record legal name, business name, entity type, and tax ID | Registration documents and tax profile | Once; update when details change |
| ☐ | Identify applicable tax authority and filing deadlines | Official tax calendar or accountant notes | Once annually |
| ☐ | Open separate income, expense, tax-reserve, and payment accounts | Bank or accounting account list | Once; review quarterly |
| ☐ | Choose bookkeeping method and accounting period | Budget Tracker settings | Once annually |
| ☐ | Confirm required estimated-tax frequency | Local tax guidance or professional advice | Once annually |
| ☐ | Create a target reserve rate for irregular payments | Written budgeting assumption | Review quarterly |

**Example planning assumption:** Reserve 25% of every freelance receipt until annual income and deductions are reviewed.

### 2. Ongoing Transaction Checklist

| Status | Review Task | Realistic Example |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Record every client payment with date, payer, invoice, and amount | Invoice F-104: $4,800 received March 12 |
| ☐ | Categorize business expenses separately from personal spending | $86 project-management software |
| ☐ | Attach receipts or invoices to each transaction | Receipt attached to $42 office-supplies purchase |
| ☐ | Flag uncertain deductions for professional review | $310 home-office electricity allocation |
| ☐ | Record deductible business mileage | 186 miles for client meeting |
| ☐ | Reconcile income and payments with bank statements | March statement matched to five deposits |
| ☐ | Move the planned percentage of each receipt to the tax-reserve account | $1,200 moved from a $4,800 payment |
| ☐ | Review uncategorized transactions weekly | Three transactions awaiting classification |

### 3. Quarterly Estimated-Tax Review

| Status | Task | Calculation or Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Total gross freelance receipts for the quarter | Sum of cleared client payments |
| ☐ | Total documented business expenses | Sum of approved expense records |
| ☐ | Review asset purchases and depreciation needs | Equipment invoices and in-use dates |
| ☐ | Calculate tentative quarterly taxable income | Receipts minus allowable expenses |
| ☐ | Apply current tax-rate estimate | Use local rates or professional guidance |
| ☐ | Subtract taxes already paid or credited | Prior estimated payments |
| ☐ | Transfer the remaining tax reserve | Record transfer in the budget |
| ☐ | Schedule or make the required payment | Save confirmation and payment record |

**Example:** Quarterly receipts of $18,000 minus documented expenses of $3,000 produce $15,000 before taxes. At a planning reserve rate of 25%, the freelancer moves $3,750 to the tax-reserve account, subject to an actual tax calculation.

### 4. Common Deduction Review

| Category | Documents to Retain | Example Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Software and subscriptions | Receipt, renewal date, business purpose | $86 project-management software |
| Office supplies | Itemized receipt and project note | $42 printer paper |
| Equipment | Invoice, serial number, business-use percentage | $1,200 monitor |
| Travel | Ticket, itinerary, mileage log, meeting purpose | $214 client-meeting train fare |
| Vehicle costs | Mileage log, fuel receipts, insurance records | 186 business miles |
| Professional services | Invoice and service description | $450 accounting consultation |
| Education | Course receipt, relevance note, duration | $320 tax-recordkeeping workshop |
| Home-office costs | Floor plan, square footage, utility bills | $310 electricity allocation |

Do not treat an expense as deductible solely because it appears in this table; confirm eligibility and documentation requirements.

### 5. Year-End Checklist

| Status | Final Task | Completion Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Reconcile all business bank and payment accounts | Final bank reconciliation |
| ☐ | Record outstanding invoices and delayed payments | Accounts-receivable report |
| ☐ | Review all potential deductions and depreciation | Annual expense schedule |
| ☐ | Confirm estimated payments and reserve balance | Payment confirmations |
| ☐ | Compare prior and current tax estimates | Estimated-tax worksheet |
| ☐ | Assemble year-end reports and source documents | Profit-and-loss and expense reports |
| ☐ | Secure records according to legal retention requirements | Archived tax folder |

**Conclusion:** Complete the checklist after every payment, review it quarterly, and finalize it before filing. The Tax Checklist creates a consistent audit trail, supports realistic reserves for irregular income, and reduces missed documents or estimated-payment deadlines.
