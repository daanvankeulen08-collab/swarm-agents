Use this log for every freelance payment and outstanding invoice. Record the **gross invoice amount**, deductions, and **actual net received**. Illustrative entries use dollars; replace them with your own currency and transactions.

**Calculation rule:**  
`Net received = Gross amount − fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, and other deductions`

For unpaid invoices, leave **Net received** at zero until money is received. Enter any deductions identified during reconciliation.

Use these formulas:

- **Gross paid:** Sum of gross amounts for paid entries.
- **Net received:** Sum of actual payments deposited.
- **Outstanding invoices:** Sum of sent, overdue, or partially paid invoices not yet collected.
- **Average payment:** Net received divided by the number of paid invoices.

## Status Guide

| Status | Meaning | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Work or invoice is still being prepared | Complete invoice details before sending |
| Sent | Invoice has been issued but no payment is recorded | Record the due date and follow-up date |
| Partially paid | Some payment has been received | Record each payment separately |
| Paid | Full gross amount has been settled | Reconcile the deposit and deductions |
| Overdue | Due date has passed without full payment | Send a reminder and document the next action |
| Void | Invoice was cancelled or duplicated | Retain the record and explain why it was voided |

## Income Tracking Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the client, project, invoice number, and payment date.
- [ ] Store the invoice and payment receipt together.
- [ ] Compare the payment with the invoice total.
- [ ] Record platform fees, taxes, refunds, and chargebacks separately.
- [ ] Confirm that the net amount matches the bank or account deposit.
- [ ] Review sent and overdue invoices weekly.
- [ ] Update partially paid invoices after every deposit.
- [ ] Add unpaid invoices to the monthly summary.
- [ ] Transfer an appropriate amount to a separate tax-savings account.
- [ ] Review totals monthly before adjusting the budget.

**Conclusion:** A complete income log distinguishes invoiced income from money actually received, exposes deductions and outstanding balances, and provides an accurate basis for budgeting during irregular freelance cash flow.
