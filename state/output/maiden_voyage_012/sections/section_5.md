### 5. Assignment Impact Rules

1. Do not treat facility credentialing deadlines as certification-board deadlines.
2. Confirm whether a hospital or clinic requires current documentation before each assignment period.
3. If renewal is pending, ask the facility coordinator—through the approved process—before scheduling work that depends on the credential.
4. Maintain a separate copy of the current credential in each relevant facility compliance record.
5. Escalate unresolved deadline risks at least 30 days before expiration, or immediately when an official notice changes a date.

**Conclusion:** The actual deadline register remains incomplete until the interpreter’s certification bodies, jurisdictions, credentials, and official expiration dates are entered. Once recorded, the dashboard, evidence checklist, internal targets, and weekly review provide a reliable system for preventing lapses and protecting hospital/clinic assignments.
