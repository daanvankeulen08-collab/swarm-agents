### 4. Assignment Checklists

**Before leaving**
- [ ] Confirm date, hours, department, modality, and check-in procedure.
- [ ] Verify interpreter ID, facility badge, parking, security, and escort requirements.
- [ ] Review relevant terminology without accessing unauthorized records.
- [ ] Charge phone, headset, battery pack, and backup device.
- [ ] Test secure technology and prepare a permitted offline backup.
- [ ] Notify the site contact of arrival or any schedule change.

**During the assignment**
- [ ] Confirm role, sequence, and parties present.
- [ ] Introduce the interpreter role and explain consecutive interpretation.
- [ ] Interpret all qualified communication accurately and completely.
- [ ] Request clarification when speech is unclear; do not summarize or advise.
- [ ] Record delays, safety concerns, or requested follow-up using minimum necessary information.
- [ ] Escalate privacy, security, or clinical-safety concerns through facility procedures.

**After the assignment**
- [ ] Record actual start/end times and unresolved clarifications.
- [ ] Secure or delete temporary notes according to policy.
- [ ] Submit encounter confirmation, timesheet, mileage, and expenses.
- [ ] Report incidents, equipment failures, or scheduling changes.
- [ ] Confirm follow-up instructions and update the next action.

**Conclusion:** A complete Assignment Notes section connects each work block with preparation, contact, documentation, safety, and follow-up—supporting reliable coverage across multiple hospital and clinic assignments.
