### Daily Checklist

**Before each assignment**

- ☐ Confirm time, modality, specialty, arrival requirements, and authorized contact.
- ☐ Check traffic, parking, badge, identification, equipment, and backup connection.
- ☐ Use a secure device and approved communication channel.
- ☐ Review relevant terminology without accessing unauthorized patient records.
- ☐ Protect professional boundaries and avoid discussing assignments on personal accounts.

**After each assignment**

- ☐ Submit encounter confirmation or required attendance record.
- ☐ Record actual hours, travel mileage, and reimbursable expenses.
- ☐ Note delays, site issues, equipment problems, and handoff instructions.
- ☐ Never store patient identifiers or clinical details in the planner.

**End of week**

- ☐ Reconcile timesheets and invoices with contracts.
- ☐ Back up the calendar and verify synchronization.
- ☐ Review workload, travel time, breaks, and upcoming deadlines.
- ☐ Request schedule changes early and confirm backup coverage when needed.

### Conclusion

A complete weekly grid balances clinical assignments with travel, preparation, administrative work, breaks, and contingency time. By combining a time-blocked schedule with assignment-readiness and daily checklists, the interpreter can reduce missed confirmations, protect confidential information, and maintain a sustainable workload across multiple hospital and clinic locations.
