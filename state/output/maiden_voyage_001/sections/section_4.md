### Invoice Checklist

#### Before Submission
- [ ] Match the invoice to the signed work order, purchase order, or client approval.
- [ ] Verify service date, hours, rate, travel, expenses, and invoice number.
- [ ] Confirm the client-specific due date and approved remittance method.
- [ ] Remove all unnecessary patient information and identifiers.
- [ ] Check for duplicate invoices and separate invoices by client or location.

#### Submission and Follow-Up
- [ ] Submit through the required portal or email address.
- [ ] Attach approved supporting records when permitted.
- [ ] Save the submitted invoice and delivery confirmation.
- [ ] Change the status from **Draft** to **Sent**, not **Paid**.
- [ ] Schedule receipt confirmation within three business days.
- [ ] Set reminders seven days before and on the due date.
- [ ] If overdue, follow up after one, three, and seven days.
- [ ] Escalate unresolved balances to the client’s billing contact.
- [ ] Record every payment, remaining balance, and next action.

**Conclusion:** A complete invoice log connects each completed assignment to supporting documentation, a confirmed due date, payment status, and a specific next action. This prevents draft invoices from being mistaken for receivables, makes partial payments transparent, and keeps overdue balances from being overlooked.
