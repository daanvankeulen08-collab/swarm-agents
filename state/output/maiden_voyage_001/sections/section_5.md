## Certification Deadlines

This tracker prevents certification lapses that could interrupt hospital or clinic assignments. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, credential, and facility; confirm every rule with the issuing organization and each client. The dates below are realistic examples, not assigned deadlines. Set internal targets at least two weeks before the verified external deadline.

### Master Deadline Register

| Priority | Credential or Requirement | Issuing Body/Client | Expiration or Due Date | Verified External Deadline | Internal Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | English medical interpreter certification | CMI or accepted equivalent | September 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | June 1, 2027 | Not started |
| High | State interpreter credential, if required | Relevant state agency | January 31, 2027 | November 30, 2026 | November 1, 2026 | Checking applicability |
| High | BLS Provider Certification | Authorized training center | January 12, 2027 | November 12, 2026 | October 15, 2026 | Not started |
| Medium | HIPAA privacy/security training | Assigned hospital or clinic | December 31, 2026 | December 15, 2026 | December 1, 2026 | Not started |
| Medium | Tuberculosis screening or risk assessment | Assigned healthcare facility | November 30, 2026 | November 15, 2026 | October 31, 2026 | In progress |
| High | Hospital interpreter privileges | Client credentialing department | Before June 15, 2026 assignment | May 15, 2026 | May 1, 2026 | Not started |
| Medium | Professional liability coverage | Insurer or broker | August 1, 2026 | July 15, 2026 | July 1, 2026 | Not started |

**Status definitions:** Not started = no action taken; In progress = requirement confirmed and underway; Submitted = application or training completed; Verified = approval received and recorded.

### Weekly Planner

| Day | Certification Action | Required Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Review all credentials expiring within 90 days. | Updated priority list |
| Tuesday | Confirm renewal rules, fees, prerequisites, and accepted credentials. | Links and requirement notes |
| Wednesday | Register for courses or exams and request employer funding if needed. | Registration confirmation |
| Thursday | Gather identification, transcripts, attestations, and continuing-education records. | Complete document checklist |
| Friday | Complete training, testing, or application preparation. | Score or draft saved securely |
| Saturday | Submit applications and follow up on missing approvals. | Receipt and follow-up date |
| Sunday | Update expiration dates, evidence locations, and next-cycle reminders. | Current tracker and backups |

### Renewal and Submission Checklist

**Twelve to sixteen weeks before expiration**

- [ ] Record the exact credential name, issuing body, jurisdiction, and expiration date.
- [ ] Confirm whether the credential is mandatory for current assignments.
- [ ] Verify the renewal cycle, continuing-education requirements, fees, examination rules, and grace periods.
- [ ] Check each hospital or clinic for separate credentialing requirements.
- [ ] Compare the credential with the names and specialties listed in the medical-interpreter profile.
- [ ] Create separate reminders for the internal target and official deadline.

**Eight to twelve weeks before expiration**

- [ ] Request supervisor approval, reimbursement, or scheduling support.
- [ ] Register for the required course, examination, or application.
- [ ] Pay fees and save the receipt.
- [ ] Schedule the exam or training with sufficient time for a retake.
- [ ] Download the current application and review every attestation.

**Four to eight weeks before expiration**

- [ ] Complete all continuing-education modules.
- [ ] Gather government identification, diploma evidence, transcripts, and prior certificates.
- [ ] Confirm name consistency across applications, identification, and payment records.
- [ ] Securely scan documents and store them in the designated encrypted folder.

**Submission week**

- [ ] Review the application for omissions or incorrect dates.
- [ ] Submit through the official channel.
- [ ] Save confirmation numbers, receipts, and submission copies.
- [ ] Add a two-week follow-up reminder.
- [ ] Notify the facility credentialing contact if approval is not received before expiration.

**After approval**

- [ ] Verify the new expiration date in the issuer portal.
- [ ] Update the master register and medical-interpreter profile.
- [ ] Archive the approval and remove obsolete documents securely.
- [ ] Calendar the next review date and any continuing-education deadline.
- [ ] Escalate immediately if an exam is failed, a renewal is denied, or approval may delay an assignment.

### Conclusion

A weekly 90-day renewal review, documented internal targets, and verified evidence create a dependable certification system. Reviewing deadlines every Monday allows freelance medical interpreters to address requirements early and avoid losing hospital or clinic privileges due to missed renewals.
